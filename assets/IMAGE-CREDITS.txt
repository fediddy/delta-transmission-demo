ABOUT SHARE-ALIKE
CC BY-SA carries a share-alike clause. Display on a website is fine. If
these were ever folded into merchandise, printed collateral, or anything
sold commercially, have a lawyer review that use first. Replacing them
with the shop's own photographs removes the question completely.

THE FORD C6 PROBLEM
--------------------
No photograph of a Ford C6 transmission exists under any free licence.
Searches across Wikimedia Commons and Openverse for C6, Ford C6, and
C6 automatic returned only Corvettes, Citroens, Audi A6s, a vertebra,
and electron micrographs. The term collides with too many other things.

Category:Ford C6 transmission exists on Commons but contains ZERO files.
That is the definitive answer: the category was created and never filled.

WHAT IS AVAILABLE FOR FORD
--------------------------
Category:Ford transmissions holds three files, and Category:Ford automatic
transmissions holds five. Of those, four are patent drawings or museum
cutaways. The one genuinely usable photograph is the Toploader above.

The other free automatic-transmission photographs available are trade-show
cutaways of Japanese units, photographed on museum stands with signage
and onlookers in frame. One was downloaded and rejected outright: it is a
Toyota/Lexus hybrid powertrain on a mirrored display pedestal with an
EXIT sign and Toyota branding legible inside the crop. Wrong brand,
wrong technology, and another company's trademark. Do not re-add it.

A real C6 has to come from the shop. They work on these daily and will
have one on a bench. A phone photograph of their own C6 beats every
free image that exists.
